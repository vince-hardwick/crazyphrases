# Word Bank Source Candidates

The default word bank should be served locally or from cache for low-latency dice-click entry assistance. Candidate source datasets to evaluate include:

- Moby Part-of-Speech Dictionary
- NLTK corpora with part-of-speech tagged word lists
- Automatically Generated Inflection Database (AGID)

Before adopting any source, verify its license, redistribution terms, packaging format, part-of-speech coverage, update path, and fit for the MVP default template's adjective and noun slots. Later participle support should be evaluated separately from the MVP adjective/noun word bank.
